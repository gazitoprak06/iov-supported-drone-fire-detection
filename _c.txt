Audit the manuscript and the release package end to end, and repair both

A full reading of the manuscript against its own artifacts, and of every
released program against its own docstring, turned up defects in both. None
changes a measured value; several would have been found by a reviewer.

In the manuscript.

Arithmetic that did not hold. The gap between the two released forest variants
was called thirteen times the sum of their deviations; it is 2.56 against 1.63,
so one and a half times. The vertical-band contribution was given as 1.73
points, which matches no pair of rows in Table 4; the forest goes 82.28 to
85.28, so 3.00. Precision was said to fall below the class prior at two sweep
settings; the artifact has four. The pipeline was called tens of milliseconds
per frame against measurements of 5.82 to 8.45. The box-count bound m <= 3
holds only in the resized configuration, where the diagnostic emits under three
boxes per image; at native resolution it emits roughly forty.

Claims the paper contradicts elsewhere. Sections 4.1 to 4.5 were said to draw
no random number, while 4.4 fits under seed 0 with a ten-seed sweep and 4.5
runs five seeds. Three cross-references sent the reader to Section 4.5 for a
qualitative demonstration of the hand-set temporal, suppression and merge
stages, which Section 4.5 does not contain and no experiment performs. The
hand-set pipeline was said to alarm on no-fire controls "on clips"; it was
never run on a clip, and the figure belongs to 202 of 251 still images. Three
visualization modes were announced two lines above a list of four.

One that mattered more than the others. The ten-seed sweep was introduced with
"the reported figure is representative rather than flattering", and the same
sentence gives seed 0 as 85.28 against a mean of 84.05 over a range topping out
at 85.28 - that is the best of the ten. Section 4.5 applies the opposite
standard to its own single-seed figure and says so. The text now states plainly
that the reported fit is the top of its distribution and that the mean is the
quantity to carry away.

Also: Table 5's caption used an undefined CSS class, described the rows that a
later addition falsified, promised two caveats and gave three, and pointed at
the fourth row for a comparison the fifth row makes; Table 1's note described a
bold cell that did not exist; the environment account gave three different
scopes in three places; the figure inventory claimed Fig. 6 was the only
measured figure and listed five image files; a sentence in Section 3.4 ended
after a semicolon; British and American spellings were mixed throughout, and
reference titles were left alone.

In the code.

The one that would have done real damage: v2_learned_baselines.py printed, on
every successful run, that Table 5 of the manuscript was stale and had to be
regenerated. A reviewer running the package would have read that the submitted
paper was out of date, from the authors' own program. It was true of a
superseded script and is not true now.

train_v3.py disabled TLS certificate verification process-wide to work around a
weight download, and its default invocation overwrote the released weights that
MANIFEST.sha256 hashes and every evaluator loads. The first is gone; the second
now refuses without --force, and multiseed_v3.py passes it and clears the
per-seed verdict cache that would otherwise have been scored against the
previous attempt's weights.

sonuclari_goster_v1_v2_v3.py - the script hocaya_goster.bat runs - raised a
NameError whenever an artifact was absent, which is the state a reader is in
before running anything. Its closing sentence asserted a comparison it never
checked; it now reads the two numbers it just printed.

clip_score_curve.py thresholded at p > 0.5 while the deployed rule is an argmax
over two logits with fire at index zero, which numpy resolves toward the first
index: the rule is p >= 0.5, and on an exact tie the curve would have disagreed
with the evaluator it exists to reproduce. It also scored a clip absent from
disk as zero and admitted it to the curve, where the t = 0.5 check could not
see it because the verdict file records no alarm for that clip either.

compare_verdict_runs.py with no argument compared the released file to itself
and overwrote the artifact with a vacuous result; it now refuses. Its
missing-clip branch printed a message and then crashed two functions later.

visualize_v3.py imported FIRE_CLASS_INDEX and then hardcoded index 0 eight
lines further down, so a class reordering would have had the heatmap explain
one class and the status bar report the other; and it cleared the alarm on a
single negative sample where the evaluator latches it, which is a different
rule than the one Section 4.5 measures.

make_curve_figure.py located the operating point on the ROC by matching the
true-positive rate, which several points share; it now reads the exact point
from the artifact. image_metrics.mcnemar left the Yates correction unclamped,
returning 1/n where the discordant cells are equal. corpus_controls.py compared
a capitalised filename prefix against a lowercase set and matched the clip
index only against a literal .mp4, either of which would have silently moved a
clip into the wrong control.

tests/test_metrics.py carried a comment asserting the double-rounded F1 that
image_metrics explicitly rejects, on a case that gives the same answer under
both conventions and so could not detect the regression. Two tests added: the
cohort where the conventions differ, 95.45 against 95.46, and the McNemar clamp.

Gate: 601 to 603 checks; tests 22 to 24.
